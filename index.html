About us

Personal attention for every student

Siddhi's Coaching Classes is near the Bharat Jyoti Bus Stop in Bibwewadi, Pune. Students come from Class 5th through the 12th Board exams, and many continue with MHT-CET, JEE, NEET and CA Foundation coaching.

Batches are kept small so teachers can work with each student one to one. Regular tests show how each student is doing, and doubt-solving sessions mean no question is left unanswered. Parents get updates at regular parent-teacher meetings.

  • One-to-one interaction
  • Chapter-wise tests
  • Doubt-solving sessions
  • Extra practice classes
  • Parent-teacher meetings
  • SSC, CBSE & ICSE boards
